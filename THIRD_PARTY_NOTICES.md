# Third-party notices

MyDataLabNotes depends on open-source scientific and notebook tooling. The exact
resolved packages are recorded in `uv.lock`; their licenses remain with their
authors and projects.

Notable direct dependencies include NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, Hypothesis,
JupyterLab, nbclient, nbformat, pytest, psutil, Ruff, and uv. No third-party
source code, figures, or datasets are vendored in this repository.

Academic and technical references are citations only. See `docs/SOURCES.md`.
